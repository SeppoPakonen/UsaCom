## CREATIVE SOURCES OF FINANCING AND FUNDING

Beyond traditional debt and equity financing, entrepreneurs can explore creative financing alternatives.

### Leasing

Leasing allows businesses to use equipment without purchasing it outright.

#### Advantages
- Preserves cash flow
- Avoids obsolescence risk
- Potential tax benefits
- Flexibility to upgrade equipment

#### Disadvantages
- Total cost may exceed purchase price
- No ownership equity
- Contractual obligations
- Potential restrictions on use

#### Types of Leasing
- Operating leases
- Capital leases
- Sale and leaseback arrangements
- Synthetic leases

### SBIR and STTR Grant Programs

The Small Business Innovation Research (SBIR) and Small Business Technology Transfer (STTR) programs provide funding for research and development.

#### SBIR Program
- For-profit companies with 500 or fewer employees
- Awards phased funding for R&D projects
- Requires matching funds from private sector
- Focus on innovative research with commercial potential

#### STTR Program
- Requires collaboration between small business and research institution
- Similar structure to SBIR but with partnership requirement
- Emphasis on technology transfer from research to commercialization

#### Advantages
- Non-dilutive funding
- No repayment required
- Supports innovation
- Validates technology potential

#### Disadvantages
- Competitive application process
- Specific eligibility requirements
- Time-consuming application process
- Limited to R&D activities

#### Application Process
1. Identify appropriate funding agency
2. Review solicitation requirements
3. Prepare proposal
4. Submit for review
5. Award notification
6. Contract negotiation and execution

### Other Grant Programs

Various organizations offer grants to entrepreneurs.

#### Government Grants
- Federal, state, and local programs
- Industry-specific funding
- Regional development grants
- Export promotion grants

#### Private Foundation Grants
- Foundations with business development missions
- Industry-specific foundations
- Community development grants
- Innovation awards

#### Advantages
- No repayment required
- No equity dilution
- Validation of concept
- Potential for follow-on funding

#### Disadvantages
- Highly competitive
- Specific use restrictions
- Reporting requirements
- Limited availability

### Strategic Partners

Strategic partnerships can provide funding and resources.

#### Advantages
- Access to capital and resources
- Industry expertise and connections
- Market access and distribution
- Shared risk and costs

#### Disadvantages
- Loss of independence
- Potential conflicts of interest
- Sharing of profits
- Complex negotiations

#### Types of Strategic Partnerships
- Joint ventures
- Licensing agreements
- Distribution partnerships
- Technology partnerships
