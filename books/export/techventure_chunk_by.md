##### Risk Prioritization Matrix

**Risk Matrix Quadrants:**
- **High Probability/High Impact**: Critical risks requiring immediate attention
- **High Probability/Low Impact**: Manage risks requiring attention
- **Low Probability/High Impact**: Monitor risks requiring contingency planning
- **Low Probability/Low Impact**: Acceptable risks requiring minimal attention

**Risk Scoring Methodology:**
- **Probability Scoring**: Score probability from 1-5
- **Impact Scoring**: Score impact from 1-5
- **Risk Score**: Calculate risk score (probability × impact)
- **Risk Ranking**: Rank risks by score
- **Risk Categories**: Group risks by category
- **Risk Ownership**: Assign risk ownership

### 33.11 Summary

Risk management is essential for technology venture success. Entrepreneurs must identify, assess, and manage risks across all aspects of their business including technology, market, financial, and operational risks. The key is to develop systematic approaches to risk management that are integrated into business processes and supported by appropriate tools and culture.

Key takeaways include:
- Risk management should be systematic and comprehensive
- Technology risks require special attention in technology ventures
- Market risks can significantly impact venture success
- Financial risks threaten venture survival
- Operational risks affect day-to-day operations
- Risk management should be proactive rather than reactive
- Risk management culture is critical for success
- Risk monitoring and reporting enable effective management

Successful technology ventures implement comprehensive risk management programs that identify potential threats, assess their likelihood and impact, and develop appropriate mitigation strategies. This proactive approach helps entrepreneurs avoid common pitfalls and increases the likelihood of venture success.
