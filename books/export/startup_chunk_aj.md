## Chapter 9: Key Partnerships and Channels

### Key Partnerships

Key partnerships describe the network of suppliers and partners that make the business model work. They are essential for resource acquisition, activity performance, and business model success.

#### Purpose of Key Partnerships
Key partnerships serve to:
- Acquire critical resources
- Perform key activities
- Reduce risk and uncertainty
- Obtain economies of scale
- Access new markets
- Share costs and resources

#### Types of Key Partnerships

##### Strategic Alliances
Strategic alliances involve partnerships between non-competitors for mutual benefit.

###### Non-Competition Partnerships
- Partnerships with non-competitors
- Shared resources and capabilities
- Mutual market expansion
- Complementary strengths
- Shared costs and risks
- Joint value creation

###### Co-opetition Partnerships
- Cooperation with competitors
- Shared market development
- Joint standard setting
- Shared technology development
- Reduced duplication
- Market expansion

##### Strategic Partnerships
Strategic partnerships involve cooperation with key suppliers or distributors.

###### Supplier Partnerships

- Cost reduction

- Market access

### Channels

Channels describe how a company communicates with customer segments to deliver a value proposition. They are critical for customer acquisition, education, and service.

#### Purpose of Channels
Channels serve to:
- Raise awareness of products and services
- Help customers evaluate value propositions
- Allow customers to purchase products and services
- Deliver value propositions to customers
- Provide post-purchase customer support
- Maintain customer relationships

#### Channel Functions

##### Awareness

- Generating interest and consideration
- Creating market presence
- Building market awareness

##### Evaluation

##### Purchase
