# The Startup Owner's Manual (continued)

## Chapter 26: Index and Key Terms

### A

#### AARRR Framework
The AARRR framework covers the customer lifecycle:
- Acquisition: How customers find the company
- Activation: How customers have a meaningful first experience
- Retention: How customers continue to find value over time
- Revenue: How the company captures value from customers
- Referral: How customers bring more customers

#### Activation
How customers have a meaningful first experience with the product or service.

##### Key Metrics
- Activation rate
- Time to first value
- Feature adoption
- User onboarding completion
- First session quality
- Engagement depth

#### Acquisition
How customers find the company and become aware of the product or service.

##### Key Metrics
- Traffic sources and volumes
- Cost per acquisition
- Channel effectiveness
- Referral rates
- Viral coefficient
- Conversion rates

#### Build-Measure-Learn Cycle
The fundamental engine of startup growth, involving building minimal viable products, measuring customer behavior, and learning from the results.

### C

#### Customer Development
A methodology for discovering and validating customer problems and solutions before building products.

##### Four Steps
- Customer Discovery
- Customer Validation
- Customer Creation
- Company Building

#### Customer Discovery
The first step in customer development, where entrepreneurs test their initial hypotheses about customers, problems, and solutions.

##### Key Activities
- Get out of the building to talk to customers
- Test problem and solution hypotheses
- Listen to customer feedback
- Iterate based on learning
- Document findings and insights

#### Customer Validation
The second step in customer development, where entrepreneurs prove that the business model is repeatable and scalable.

##### Key Activities
- Test the sales model with real customers
- Validate marketing messages and positioning
- Confirm business model assumptions
- Train sales and marketing teams
- Establish metrics and tracking systems

#### Customer Creation
The third step in customer development, where entrepreneurs focus on scaling the business through marketing and sales execution.

##### Key Activities
- Execute proven marketing and sales processes
- Build demand through marketing programs
- Scale sales operations efficiently
- Expand market reach systematically
- Optimize customer acquisition costs

#### Company Building
The fourth step in customer development, where entrepreneurs transition from startup to established company by building formal processes and scaling the organization.

##### Key Activities
- Build formal organizational structures
- Establish management systems
- Scale the organization appropriately
- Implement management controls
- Prepare for growth or exit

#### Channel Strategy
The approach to delivering products to customers through various distribution channels.

##### Types of Channels
- Direct sales

- Online marketplaces
- Wholesale channels
- Partner channels
- Digital channels
