## Chapter 10: Getting Financing or Funding

### Opening Profile—Bizooki: Raising Money Through a Variety of Sources

Bizooki represents the importance of exploring multiple funding sources for new ventures. The company's founders successfully raised money through a combination of personal savings, angel investors, and strategic partnerships, which contributed to their success.

The company operates in the technology sector, where multiple funding rounds are often necessary. Bizooki's funding approach included:

- Bootstrapping in the early stages
- Angel investor funding for growth
- Strategic partnerships for additional capital
- Careful financial management to preserve cash
- Clear milestones to attract investors

The company's success demonstrates the importance of having a diversified funding strategy.

## THE IMPORTANCE OF GETTING FINANCING OR FUNDING

New ventures require financing to:
- Develop products and services
- Hire employees
- Purchase equipment and inventory
- Market and sell products
- Support operations until profitability

### Why Most New Ventures Need Funding

Most new ventures need external funding because:
- Start-up costs exceed founders' personal resources
- Revenue generation takes time
- Growth requires additional investment
- Competition requires adequate capitalization
- Market opportunities require quick action

### Sources of Personal Financing

Before seeking external funding, entrepreneurs typically use personal resources:

#### Personal Savings
Using personal savings is the most common source of initial funding.

#### Credit Cards
Credit cards provide quick access to capital but carry high interest rates.

#### Home Equity Loans
Home equity loans offer lower interest rates but put personal assets at risk.

#### Retirement Accounts
Some retirement accounts can be used for business funding with special arrangements.

#### Friends and Family
Friends and family often provide initial funding based on personal relationships.

### WHAT WENT WRONG? Webvan: How Having Too Much Money Can Be Just as Harmful as Having Too Little

This section examines how Webvan, an online grocery delivery company, failed despite raising substantial funding. The case illustrates that having too much money can be harmful if not used wisely.

Webvan raised over $1 billion but failed because:
- Rapid expansion without proven unit economics
- Overinvestment in infrastructure before market validation
- Poor cash flow management
- Inefficient use of capital
- Lack of focus on profitability

The lesson is that the amount of funding matters less than how it's used.

### Preparing to Raise Debt or Equity Financing

Before approaching investors, entrepreneurs should prepare:

#### Business Plan
A comprehensive business plan that demonstrates market opportunity and execution strategy.

#### Financial Projections
Detailed financial projections that show path to profitability.

#### Market Analysis
Thorough market analysis that demonstrates opportunity size and competitive dynamics.

#### Management Team
Evidence of a capable management team that can execute the plan.

#### Milestones
Clear milestones that demonstrate progress and justify funding requests.

#### Legal Structure
Proper legal structure and documentation to facilitate investment.

#### Intellectual Property
Protection of key intellectual property assets.

The preparation process itself often improves the venture's prospects for success.
