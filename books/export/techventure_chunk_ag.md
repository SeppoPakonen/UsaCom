## Chapter 5: Developing an Effective Business Model (continued)

### 5.6 Summary

Successful innovative firms strive to time their entry into markets. They balance a sense of urgency with a deliberate buildup to action. Working with partners—firms and individuals—most firms can enhance their capabilities and strengths for creativity, invention, and innovation. Almost all firms build an innovation strategy that strives to provide them with a sustainable action plan.

#### Key Takeaways from the Chapter

**First-Mover Advantages and Challenges**
- A first-mover strategy can lead to significant benefits in an emerging market, but is not a guarantee of success.
- First movers may gain advantages such as:
  - Brand recognition and customer loyalty
  - Network effects and market dominance
  - Learning curve advantages
  - Resource capture
  - Market definition
  - Standard setting

However, first movers also face challenges including:
- Pioneer costs and market education expenses
- Technology obsolescence risks
- Customer resistance to new solutions
- Competitive imitation
- High development costs
- Market uncertainty

### Case Study: AgraQuest

AgraQuest provides an excellent example of successful innovation strategy implementation. The company focused on developing biological crop protection products that could control pests and diseases while being environmentally friendly.

#### AgraQuest's Innovation Strategy
- **Proprietary Processes**: Developed unique methods for biological solutions
- **Patent Portfolio**: Built comprehensive intellectual property protection
- **Microbe Innovation**: Focused on beneficial microorganisms
- **Natural Product Compounds**: Developed novel natural solutions
- **Mixture Development**: Created effective compound combinations

#### Patent Strategy
AgraQuest's innovation strategy was based upon proprietary processes and patents. The patents covered:
- The microbe and its use
- Novel natural product compounds
- Effective mixture formulations
- Application methods
- Manufacturing processes
- Market applications

#### Acquisition and Integration
Bayer CropScience of Germany acquired AgraQuest for $425 million in 2012. The acquisition provided several benefits:
- **Technology Platform**: Access to green product technology
- **Pest Control Solutions**: Broad spectrum pest and disease control
- **Integrated Programs**: Pest management programs for farmers
- **Resistance Management**: Minimization of pesticide-resistant crops
- **Yield Optimization**: Maximization of crop yields
- **Sustainability**: Environmentally friendly solutions

#### Lessons from AgraQuest
- **Focus on Unique Value**: Develop distinctive solutions
- **Intellectual Property Protection**: Build strong IP portfolio
- **Strategic Partnerships**: Consider acquisition as exit strategy
- **Market Timing**: Enter market when ready
- **Innovation Continuity**: Maintain innovation pipeline
- **Value Proposition**: Create clear customer value
