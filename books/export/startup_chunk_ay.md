## Chapter 24: Enterprise Sales and Influence Mapping

### The Enterprise Sales Process

Enterprise sales is a complex process that involves multiple stakeholders, long sales cycles, and sophisticated decision-making processes. Understanding enterprise sales is critical for startups selling to large organizations.

#### Purpose of Enterprise Sales
Enterprise sales serves to:
- Sell to large organizations
- Navigate complex decision processes
- Build long-term customer relationships
- Generate substantial revenue
- Establish market presence
- Enable business model success

#### Enterprise Sales vs. SMB Sales
Enterprise sales differs from SMB sales in:
- Longer sales cycles
- Multiple decision makers
- Complex evaluation processes
- Higher transaction values
- More sophisticated requirements
- Greater relationship importance

#### Creating Influence Maps
Develop influence maps to navigate enterprise sales.

##### Influence Map Components
- Decision maker identification
- Influence relationship mapping
- Power dynamic analysis
- Decision process mapping
- Stakeholder interest assessment
- Relationship strength evaluation

##### Mapping Process
1. Identify the primary champion
2. Map their reporting relationships
3. Identify influencers and gatekeepers
4. Assess stakeholder interests
5. Evaluate relationship strengths
6. Plan stakeholder engagement

#### Example Influence Map

##### Scenario
CFO Karen Rogers is the earlyvangelist. It's her decision to buy early, buggy, unfinished enterprise financial software. Her two direct reports, Neil and Suzanne, will influence her. Before Karen buys, two in-house competitors must be neutralized: financial-modeling and tools-development staffers in sales and IT departments. Their bosses (the CIO and sales VP) will weigh in before Karen gets CEO sign-off.

##### Map Elements
- Primary champion: CFO Karen Rogers
- Direct influencers: Neil and Suzanne
- Internal competitors: Financial modeling staff, Tools development staff
- Senior influencers: CIO, Sales VP
- Ultimate decision maker: CEO
- Process: Multiple approvals required
