### 12.9 Seven Principles of Trust

Building trust within entrepreneurial teams is fundamental to organizational success. According to research, trust follows seven key principles that guide effective team dynamics.

#### Principle 1: Trust is Not Blind
Trust should be based on knowledge and experience rather than assumption.

##### Key Elements
- **Knowledge-Based Trust**: Trust individuals you know well
- **Observation**: Observe people in action over time
- **Goal Alignment**: Ensure commitment to shared goals
- **Experience**: Build trust through shared experiences
- **Assessment**: Evaluate trustworthiness systematically
- **Validation**: Confirm trust through consistent behavior

##### Implementation
- Conduct thorough reference checks
- Observe performance in various situations
- Assess commitment to team goals
- Build trust gradually over time
- Validate trust through actions
- Monitor trust levels continuously

#### Principle 2: Trust Needs Boundaries
Trust should be limited to specific areas where individuals have proven reliability.

##### Boundary Setting
- **Area-Specific Trust**: Trust in specific domains
- **Gradual Expansion**: Expand trust areas gradually
- **Competency-Based**: Trust based on demonstrated competency
- **Responsibility Limits**: Define responsibility boundaries
- **Performance Standards**: Set clear performance expectations
- **Monitoring Systems**: Implement oversight mechanisms

##### Boundary Management
- Identify areas of proven reliability
- Set clear expectations for each area
- Monitor performance within boundaries
- Gradually expand boundaries based on performance
- Maintain oversight in all areas
- Adjust boundaries based on results

#### Principle 3: Trust Requires Constant Learning
Teams must continuously develop capabilities to maintain trust.

##### Learning Requirements
- **Self-Renewal**: Continuous personal development
- **Skill Development**: Ongoing capability building
- **Adaptation**: Ability to adapt to change
- **Knowledge Sharing**: Exchange of learning and insights
- **Innovation**: Continuous improvement mindset
- **Growth Mindset**: Embrace learning opportunities

##### Learning Implementation
- Provide ongoing training opportunities
- Encourage skill development
- Foster innovation and creativity
- Create knowledge sharing systems
- Support experimentation and learning
- Reward learning and improvement

#### Principle 4: Trust is Tough
When trust is misplaced, decisive action is required to maintain organizational integrity.

##### Accountability Measures
- **Performance Standards**: Clear expectations
- **Consequence Systems**: Results-based consequences
- **Corrective Action**: Address performance issues
- **Removal**: Remove non-performers when necessary
- **Reassignment**: Move people to appropriate roles
- **Boundary Adjustment**: Restrict responsibilities when needed

##### Tough Decisions
- Address performance problems promptly
- Remove people who don't meet expectations
- Reassign individuals to appropriate roles
- Severely curtail boundaries for unreliable people
- Maintain organizational standards
- Protect team integrity

#### Principle 5: Trust Needs Bonding
Teams require strong interpersonal connections to function effectively.

##### Bonding Activities
- **Team Building**: Structured team development activities
- **Shared Experiences**: Common challenges and successes
- **Collaboration**: Working together on projects
- **Communication**: Open and honest dialogue
- **Mutual Support**: Supporting each other's success
- **Common Goals**: Shared objectives and outcomes

##### Bonding Development
- Create opportunities for interaction
- Facilitate team building activities
- Encourage collaboration on projects
- Promote open communication
- Support mutual assistance
- Align goals and incentives

#### Principle 6: Trust Needs Touch
Personal contact is essential for building and maintaining trust.

##### Personal Contact Benefits
- **Face-to-Face Interaction**: Direct personal communication
- **Body Language**: Non-verbal communication cues
- **Relationship Building**: Personal connection development
- **Trust Verification**: Direct assessment of trustworthiness
- **Bonding**: Strengthening interpersonal connections
- **Communication**: Clear and effective communication

##### Contact Strategies
- Schedule regular in-person meetings
- Facilitate team gatherings
- Encourage face-to-face communication
- Build personal relationships
- Verify trust through direct interaction
- Strengthen bonds through contact

#### Principle 7: Trust Has to be Earned
Organizations must demonstrate trustworthiness to earn trust from employees.

##### Organizational Trustworthiness
- **Consistency**: Reliable behavior and decisions
- **Transparency**: Open communication and information sharing
- **Integrity**: Honesty and ethical behavior
- **Follow-Through**: Keeping commitments and promises
- **Fairness**: Equitable treatment of all employees
- **Accountability**: Taking responsibility for actions

##### Earning Trust
- Demonstrate consistent behavior
- Share information openly
- Act with integrity consistently
- Keep all commitments made
- Treat all employees fairly
- Take responsibility for mistakes
- Show genuine concern for employees
- Provide support when needed
- Recognize and reward contributions
- Maintain confidentiality when promised
