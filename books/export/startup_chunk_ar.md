# The Startup Owner's Manual (continued)

## Chapter 17: Earlyvangelists - The Most Important Customers

### The Role of Earlyvangelists

Earlyvangelists are the most important customers for startups. They are visionary customers who buy unfinished and untested products because they believe in the startup's vision and want to be first to experience the solution.

#### Definition of Earlyvangelists
Earlyvangelists are:
- Visionary customers who see the potential
- Early adopters willing to take risks
- Customers who buy unfinished products
- Advocates who spread the word
- Partners in product development
- Critical for startup success

#### Characteristics of Earlyvangelists
Earlyvangelists typically have:
- Vision to see potential
- Tolerance for imperfection
- Need for innovative solutions
- Influence in their networks
- Patience with development
- Investment in success

#### Importance of Earlyvangelists
Earlyvangelists are critical because they:
- Provide initial revenue
- Give product feedback
- Validate market demand
- Create word-of-mouth marketing
- Help refine the product
- Enable startup survival

### Identifying Earlyvangelists

#### Ideal Customer Profile
Earlyvangelists match the ideal customer profile:
- Have the problem being solved
- Have budget to pay for solutions
- Have authority to make purchase decisions
- Are early adopters of technology
- Are influential in their networks
- Are willing to provide feedback

#### Customer Segmentation
Focus on specific customer segments:
- Early adopters
- Visionary customers
- Influential users
- Problem-aware customers
- Budget-conscious customers
- Decision-making customers

#### Customer Discovery
Use customer discovery to identify earlyvangelists:
- Interview potential customers
- Understand their problems
- Assess their needs
- Evaluate their budget
- Determine their authority
- Identify their influence

### Common Earlyvangelist Mistakes

#### Wrong Customer Focus
Targeting the wrong customers.

##### Solutions
- Focus on early adopters
- Target visionary customers
- Identify problem-aware customers
- Look for budget availability
- Seek decision-making authority
- Find influential users

#### Poor Communication
Failing to maintain regular communication.

##### Solutions
- Establish regular check-ins
- Provide consistent updates
- Request ongoing feedback
- Address concerns promptly
- Share success stories
- Build personal relationships

#### Inadequate Support
Not providing sufficient support.

##### Solutions
- Offer dedicated support
- Provide quick responses
- Be proactive in problem-solving
- Focus on customer success
- Build support relationships
- Enable customer success

#### Ignoring Feedback
Not acting on customer feedback.

##### Solutions
- Actively solicit feedback
- Implement valuable suggestions
- Communicate changes made
- Show appreciation for input
- Integrate feedback into planning
- Demonstrate value of feedback

#### Premature Scaling
Scaling before establishing earlyvangelist relationships.

##### Solutions
- Focus on earlyvangelist success
- Build strong relationships
- Iterate based on feedback
- Prove product-market fit
- Establish reliable processes
- Scale gradually

#### Poor Expectation Management
Setting unrealistic expectations.

##### Solutions
- Be honest about limitations
- Set realistic timelines
- Communicate development plans
- Manage feature requests
- Provide transparent updates
- Maintain trust relationships
