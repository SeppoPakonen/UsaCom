## SOURCES OF EQUITY FUNDING

Equity funding involves selling ownership stakes in the company in exchange for capital. Common sources include:

### Personal Investment
Using personal savings and assets to fund the business.

#### Advantages
- Maintains control
- No repayment obligation
- Demonstrates commitment to investors
- No interest costs

#### Disadvantages
- Limited by personal resources
- Personal financial risk
- May limit growth potential

### Friends and Family
Early funding from personal contacts.

#### Advantages
- Often more patient capital
- May accept lower returns
- Easier to obtain initially

#### Disadvantages
- Potential relationship strain
- May lack business expertise
- Limited funding amounts

### Business Angels

Business angels are wealthy individuals who invest in early-stage companies in exchange for ownership equity or convertible debt.

#### Characteristics of Business Angels
- Typically accredited investors
- Often have business experience
- Provide mentorship and guidance
- Invest smaller amounts than VCs
- Faster decision-making process

#### Angel Investment Process
1. Pitch presentation
2. Due diligence
3. Term sheet negotiation
4. Legal documentation
5. Closing and funding

#### What Angels Look For
- Strong management team
- Large market opportunity
- Unique value proposition
- Clear exit strategy
- Reasonable valuation

#### Advantages of Angel Funding
- Speed of decision-making
- Business expertise and mentorship
- Networks and connections
- Less formal reporting requirements
- Potential for follow-on investment

#### Disadvantages of Angel Funding
- Loss of ownership and control
- Potential for conflicting advice
- May require board seat
- Possible interference in operations

### Venture Capital

Venture capital firms pool money from various sources to invest in high-growth potential companies.

#### Characteristics of VC Firms
- Institutional investors
- Professional management
- Focus on high-growth sectors
- Larger investment amounts
- Formal investment process

#### VC Investment Process
1. Initial screening
2. Management presentation
3. Due diligence
4. Term sheet negotiation
5. Legal documentation
6. Closing and funding

#### What VCs Look For
- Scalable business model
- Large addressable market
- Strong management team
- Competitive advantage
- Clear path to exit

#### Advantages of VC Funding
- Large amounts of capital
- Professional guidance
- Industry connections
- Credibility and validation
- Follow-on investment capability

#### Disadvantages of VC Funding
- Significant loss of control
- Pressure for rapid growth
- Potential for forced exits
- Complex legal requirements
- Dilution of ownership

### Initial Public Offering

An initial public offering (IPO) involves selling shares to the public for the first time.

#### Advantages
- Large amounts of capital
- Liquidity for investors
- Enhanced credibility
- Currency for acquisitions
- Employee incentives

#### Disadvantages
- High costs and complexity
- Regulatory requirements
- Public scrutiny
- Loss of privacy
- Short-term pressure

## SOURCES OF DEBT FINANCING

Debt financing involves borrowing money that must be repaid with interest.

### Commercial Banks

Traditional banks provide various types of business loans.

#### Types of Bank Loans
- Term loans
- Lines of credit
- Equipment financing
- SBA guaranteed loans

#### Advantages
- Retain ownership and control
- Tax deductibility of interest
- Predictable repayment schedule
- Established relationships

#### Disadvantages
- Collateral requirements
- Personal guarantees
- Cash flow requirements
- Restrictions on operations

### SBA Guaranteed Loans

The Small Business Administration guarantees loans made by banks.

#### Advantages
- Lower down payments
- Longer repayment terms
- Lower interest rates
- Easier qualification

#### Disadvantages
- Lengthy application process
- Stringent requirements
- Personal guarantees required
- Use restrictions

### Other Sources of Debt Financing

#### Equipment Leasing
Leasing equipment instead of purchasing outright.

#### Trade Credit
Payment terms from suppliers.

#### Factoring
Selling accounts receivable to a third party.

#### Microloans
Small loans from nonprofit organizations.

This section highlights how John and Caprial Pence used SBA guaranteed loans to fund their business. The couple's experience demonstrates:

#### Benefits of SBA Loans
- More favorable terms than conventional loans
- Lower down payment requirements
- Longer repayment periods
- Support for entrepreneurs who might not qualify for conventional financing

#### Application Process
- Thorough business plan preparation
- Financial documentation
- Collateral assessment
- Personal guarantee requirements

- Strong business plan
- Good credit history
