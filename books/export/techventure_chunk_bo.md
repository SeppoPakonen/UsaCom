##### Technology Licensing
Technology licensing allows companies to use technology developed by others in exchange for compensation.

**Types of Licenses:**
- **Exclusive License**: Only licensee can use the technology
- **Non-Exclusive License**: Multiple licensees can use the technology
- **Sole License**: Licensee and licensor can use the technology
- **Field of Use**: License for specific applications
- **Territory License**: License for specific geographic areas
- **Sublicensing Rights**: Rights to sublicense to others

**Licensing Benefits:**
- **Revenue Generation**: Generate revenue from technology
- **Risk Reduction**: Reduce commercialization risk
- **Resource Efficiency**: Efficient resource utilization
- **Market Access**: Access to licensee's market
- **Complementary Capabilities**: Access to licensee's capabilities
- **Speed to Market**: Faster market entry

**Licensing Challenges:**
- **Control Loss**: Loss of control over technology
- **Revenue Sharing**: Sharing revenue with licensees
- **Relationship Management**: Managing licensee relationships
- **Performance Monitoring**: Monitoring licensee performance
- **Compliance**: Ensuring license compliance
- **Enforcement**: Enforcing license terms

**Licensing Process:**
- **Technology Assessment**: Assess technology for licensing
- **Market Identification**: Identify potential licensees
- **Valuation**: Value the technology
- **Negotiation**: Negotiate license terms
- **Documentation**: Complete license documentation
- **Management**: Manage license relationships

### 23.8 Summary

Technology commercialization and innovation management are critical for entrepreneurial success. Entrepreneurs must develop systematic approaches to technology development, innovation management, and technology commercialization. The key is to balance innovation with execution, manage technology risks effectively, and create innovation-friendly environments that support continuous improvement and growth.

Key takeaways include:
- Technology strategy should align with business strategy
- Innovation management requires systematic approaches
- Technology commercialization requires market focus
- Innovation culture supports continuous improvement
- Technology assessment guides investment decisions
- Innovation metrics enable performance management
- Technology partnerships can accelerate development
- Risk management protects innovation investments

Successful technology ventures invest in innovation capabilities while maintaining focus on commercialization and value creation. They build innovation-friendly cultures, implement systematic innovation processes, and create innovation-friendly environments that support continuous learning and improvement.
