#### Technology Assessment Framework

**TRL Scale:**
- **TRL 1**: Basic principles observed
- **TRL 2**: Technology concept formulated
- **TRL 3**: Experimental proof of concept
- **TRL 4**: Technology validated in lab
- **TRL 5**: Technology validated in relevant environment
- **TRL 6**: Technology demonstrated in relevant environment

- **TRL 8**: Actual system completed and qualified
- **TRL 9**: Actual system proven through successful mission operations

**TRL Assessment Process:**
- **Current Assessment**: Assess current TRL
- **Gap Analysis**: Identify gaps to required TRL

### 26.8 Summary

Technology commercialization and innovation management are critical for entrepreneurial success. Entrepreneurs must develop systematic approaches to technology development, portfolio management, and commercialization. The key is to balance innovation with execution, manage technology risks effectively, and create innovation-friendly environments that support continuous improvement and growth.

Key takeaways include:
- Technology strategy should align with business strategy
- Innovation management requires systematic approaches
- Technology commercialization requires market focus
- Innovation culture supports continuous improvement
- Technology assessment guides investment decisions
- Innovation metrics enable performance management
- Technology partnerships can accelerate development
- Risk management protects innovation investments

Successful technology ventures invest in innovation capabilities while maintaining focus on commercialization and value creation. They build innovation-friendly cultures, implement systematic innovation processes, and create innovation-friendly environments that support continuous learning and improvement.

The technology commercialization process should be systematic and well-planned, with clear milestones and metrics. Entrepreneurs who approach technology commercialization strategically are more likely to achieve sustainable success and create value for all stakeholders.
