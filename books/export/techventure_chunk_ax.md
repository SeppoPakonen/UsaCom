## Chapter 21: Case Study - Method: Designing for Sustainability and Growth

### The Method Case Study

Method represents an excellent example of how technology ventures can successfully integrate sustainability into their business models while achieving significant growth and market success.

#### Company Background
Method was founded with a clear mission to create environmentally friendly cleaning products that were both effective and aesthetically appealing. The company challenged the traditional cleaning product industry by combining sustainability with design excellence.

#### Sustainability Focus
Method's commitment to sustainability is evident in multiple aspects of their business:

##### Material Innovation
- **Microfiber Technology**: Adoption of microfiber materials for cleaning products
- **Biobased Materials**: Development of PLA (polylactic acid) nonwoven cloth
- **Sustainable Sourcing**: Focus on environmentally responsible materials
- **Durability**: Creation of reusable and washable products
- **Waste Reduction**: Elimination of single-use disposable products
- **Circular Economy**: Design for reuse and recycling

##### Environmental Philosophy

### 21.4 Summary

Method's success demonstrates how technology ventures can integrate sustainability into their business models while achieving significant growth and market success. The company's focus on sustainable materials, design excellence, and market education created a unique competitive position in the cleaning products industry.

Key lessons include:
- Sustainability can be a source of competitive advantage
- Design excellence differentiates products in the market
- Market education is often necessary for new approaches
- Innovation investment is critical for sustainable success
- Premium pricing can support sustainability investments
- Long-term thinking is essential for sustainable business models

Entrepreneurs can learn from Method's approach to integrating sustainability into their business models while maintaining focus on performance, design, and market success. The key is to balance sustainability with other business objectives and create value for all stakeholders.
