#### Risk Assessment Framework

##### Risk Probability Assessment
Assessing the likelihood of risks occurring.

**Probability Categories:**
- **Rare**: Less than 1% chance of occurrence
- **Unlikely**: 1-10% chance of occurrence
- **Possible**: 10-30% chance of occurrence
- **Likely**: 30-70% chance of occurrence
- **Almost Certain**: 70-99% chance of occurrence
- **Certain**: 99%+ chance of occurrence

**Probability Assessment Methods:**
- **Historical Data**: Use historical data when available
- **Expert Judgment**: Use expert judgment and experience
- **Statistical Analysis**: Use statistical analysis methods
- **Simulation**: Use simulation techniques
- **Scenario Analysis**: Use scenario analysis
- **Sensitivity Analysis**: Use sensitivity analysis

##### Risk Impact Assessment
Assessing the potential impact of risks.

**Impact Categories:**
- **Insignificant**: Minimal impact on objectives
- **Minor**: Minor impact on objectives
- **Moderate**: Moderate impact on objectives
- **Major**: Major impact on objectives
- **Severe**: Severe impact on objectives
- **Catastrophic**: Catastrophic impact on objectives

**Impact Dimensions:**
- **Financial Impact**: Financial consequences
- **Operational Impact**: Operational consequences
- **Reputational Impact**: Reputational consequences
- **Strategic Impact**: Strategic consequences
- **Compliance Impact**: Compliance consequences
- **Competitive Impact**: Competitive consequences

#### Risk Matrix Development

##### Risk Scoring
Creating a risk matrix to prioritize risks.

**Risk Score Calculation:**
Risk Score = Probability × Impact

**Risk Matrix Quadrants:**
- **High Risk**: High probability and high impact
- **Medium Risk**: Medium probability and medium impact
- **Low Risk**: Low probability and low impact
- **Monitor Risk**: Low probability but high impact

**Risk Prioritization:**
- **Critical Risks**: Immediate attention required
- **High Risks**: Significant attention required
- **Medium Risks**: Regular monitoring required
- **Low Risks**: Minimal monitoring required
- **Acceptable Risks**: Acceptable within tolerance
- **Unacceptable Risks**: Require immediate mitigation

### 27.4 Risk Response Strategies

**When to Use Avoidance:**
- **High Probability/High Impact**: Very dangerous risks
- **No Value Creation**: Risks that don't create value
- **Alternative Options**: When alternatives exist
- **Regulatory Requirements**: When required by regulation
- **Ethical Considerations**: When ethically required
- **Resource Constraints**: When resources are limited

**Avoidance Benefits:**
- **Complete Elimination**: Complete risk elimination
- **Certainty**: Certainty of no risk occurrence
- **Resource Allocation**: Resources freed for other uses
- **Focus**: Focus on safer opportunities
- **Compliance**: Regulatory compliance

### 27.13 Summary

Risk management is essential for technology venture success. Entrepreneurs must identify, assess, and manage risks across all aspects of their business including technology, market, financial, and operational risks. The key is to develop systematic approaches to risk management that are integrated into business processes and supported by appropriate tools and culture.

Key takeaways include:
- Risk management should be systematic and comprehensive
- Technology risks require special attention in technology ventures
- Market risks can significantly impact venture success
- Financial risks threaten venture survival
- Operational risks affect day-to-day operations
- Risk management should be proactive rather than reactive
- Risk management culture is critical for success
- Risk monitoring and reporting enable effective management

Successful technology ventures implement comprehensive risk management programs that identify potential threats, assess their likelihood and impact, and develop appropriate mitigation strategies. This proactive approach helps entrepreneurs avoid common pitfalls and increases the likelihood of venture success.
